The gift

…became the profession

…like for many people she works with.

Orama's clear spiritual vision as well as her holistic, empathic and practical approach has guided healers, consultants and creatives. At the centre of her work is helping others to see their soul and path clearly, to find self-validation, to build and expand their businesses around integrity, to hold greater visions for their life, and to gently but powerfully grow out of living or relationship situations that no longer serve them.

With over ten years of experience, Orama — a former journalist, art director and creative — has facilitated more than 1,000 one-on-one consultation and healing sessions and hundreds of Akashic readings.

She has extensive experience as teacher and trainer in workshops and trainings: participated as trainee in over 200 seminar days, and assisted her own teachers on more than 100 days.

As trainer for Spiritual Teachers in the tradition of Agni she is serving in the spiritual school of her own root teacher.

As psycho-spiritual coach Orama brings together nervous system and neuroplasticity knowledge with her ability to read patterns and symptoms, and hands-on practical advice for everyday life.

To awaken the divine spark in others through spiritual wisdom, word, heart-to-heart contact and embodiment practices is Orama's joy and mission.

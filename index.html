What Is Spiritual Guidance?

Seeing Your Gifts Is My Gift

Like for many people I work with, my gift became my profession. In my case, that is seeing how your essence wants to transmit into your life — helping you to receive what you truly wish for, whether that is more freedom, health, money or connection, by uncovering your gifts and releasing what is blocking them.

For over ten years — but practically my whole life — I have been guiding others on their path in one-on-one work, Akashic readings and group work and trainings, with clear spiritual vision and a holistic, empathic and practical approach.

People find me when they want to dive deeper into understanding and expressing who they are. Many of them are healing practitioners, consultants and creatives.

At the centre of my work is connecting you to your soul and your path, supporting you to find self-validation, to build and expand your business around integrity, and to hold greater visions for your life.

Since unfolding my business I have given more than 2,000 one-on-one consultations and healing sessions, gained extensive experience as a teacher — facilitating myself as well as assisting my own teachers — and made hundreds of Akashic readings.

Akashic readings from me connect you to your soul: its energy, its colours, its shapes, its purpose, power and origin — to bring you back home and remember yourself, through various lifetimes and in this one. A reading is a spiritual initiation, a deep call to move closer to who you are and what you want to bring into this world. It reminds you of your magic and shows you the directions in life that are fortunate for you.

I am specialised in reading and initiating seminars, trainings and offers for practitioners, healers and trainers, from their own knowledge and energy.

As a trainer I offer various advanced seminars and workshops. Advanced means they are for those who not only want to participate and learn something new, but who want to lead themselves and others on a deep journey of homecoming, healing, fulfilment, inspiration and power.

I serve in the school of my own root teacher, as a trainer for Spiritual Teachers in the Tradition of Agni.

In one-on-one work I bring together my ability to read patterns, symptoms and qualities with somatic knowledge and hands-on practical advice for everyday life. Many supportive levels come together for your growth and healing, all simple yet powerful: insight, messages from the subtle realms, energetic transmission and clearing, herbs, and structural approaches for your life.
